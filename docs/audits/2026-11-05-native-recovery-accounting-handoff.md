## Diagnosis and source trace

The contaminated granted server profile had 91.44 CPU seconds. `unresolvedLocalOutsideHistory` accounted for 19.07 cumulative seconds (20.86%); `numTrackedPackets` 19.71 (21.56%, including that scan); `nativeEndpoint.do` 6.81 (7.45%); shared progress 3.89 (4.25%, overlapping control); and `CompleteLocalPacket` 5.84 (6.39%). These overlapping costs are not additive. Approximately 55 ms of sampled mutex delay was attributed to endpoint-control mutex stacks, which supplies little evidence for dominant lock blocking but does not exclude scheduling effects. No intervention has shown that eliminating this scan alone restores the ordinary cost target.

The exact invariant is the existing shared capacity count: retained current history slots, including holes, plus unresolved local-token identities not represented by those slots. Its owner is `sentPacketHandler` and its histories in the connection context. Registration (`local_completion.go:158–174`), central aggregation (`sent_packet_handler.go:1304–1324`) and send admission (`:1350–1377`) expose the scan on repeated hot queries. The count cannot be replaced by just live packets or map length: either loses history holes or double-counts represented identities.

History insertion and all direct storage mutation are in `sent_packet_history.go`. `Remove` and `DeclareLost` can clear a packet pointer without removing the physical slot; `cleanupStart` advances the physical prefix; `releaseRetained` discards a path-zero span. `CompleteLocalPacket` validates a token then deletes its map entry before checking contradiction or whether protocol retirement already cleared its packet. Retry and Initial/Handshake disposal replace/remove a whole space; 0-RTT rejection instead removes packets from the shared application history. Close replaces current histories while retaining unresolved token identities. Path quiescence waits for local records before reclamation, and aggregate capacity includes retained paths. These distinctions drive the N4 matrix.

No repeated-root approach stop is inferred from the failed STREAM pair and profile attribution: the latter is not a second semantic invariant counterexample, and neither is a RAS finding. The accepted performance failure made an untraced adjacent owner material; this handoff widens only the representation of that owner's capacity accounting, preserving recovery policy.
