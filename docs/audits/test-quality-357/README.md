# Test quality, fixture lifecycle, and assertion validity

Audit source: `07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6`. Specification: [issue #357](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/issues/357). [Accepted contract](../../agents/test-quality-357.md) · [Complete inventory](inventory.json) · [Evidence and execution-mode reconciliation](evidence.md).

## Result and how to read it

The audit identifies seven repair candidates: shared proxy completion, fixture socket ownership, worker-side fatal assertions, a vacuous greasing-position assertion, mandatory statistical assertions, unproven blocked-read readiness, and optional qlog filename collisions. Source inspection establishes several concrete mechanisms; none was newly reproduced by running tests. Existing intermittent failures retain their own evidence and budgets. The suite is not certified flake-free, and finding no issue in an initial screen is not a correctness verdict.

The inventory accounts for all 327 tracked `_test.go` files and additional shared Go infrastructure: 356 Go files in total, of which 321 are maintained, 22 are generated mocks, and 13 are frozen experiment fixtures. Maintained declarations comprise 1,646 Test entrypoints, 47 benchmarks, 10 fuzz targets, 11 setup functions and 836 helper/method declarations. These are source counts across platforms/modules, not numbers runnable on one host or counts of expanded subtests. Non-Go infrastructure is accounted separately; the historical audit subtree is pinned as an explicit exclusion.

Every maintained declaration has a seven-dimensional initial assessment, source span and status. `initial-screen` means parser-backed, rule-driven triage of oracle sites, scenario domains, local declaration references, concurrency/time/randomness/environment signals and source-family context. It does **not** mean every predicate was independently checked against production. `source-inspected` means the particular bounded scope in the evidence document was examined more closely; `finding` links a precise concern, not a verdict on every scenario in that function. Deferred questions are explicit per member. The inventory retains top-level tests as scenario groups, with nested Run/range sites and same-package declaration references; the pinned source owns dynamic table membership. Benchmark and fuzz oracles are assessed according to their purpose rather than requiring unit-test-style equality assertions.

The census is complete at the declared source boundary; deeper semantic inspection remains selective. A fully manual predicate-by-predicate audit, mutation sensitivity for all tests, native execution on every platform, and probabilistic reliability measurement remain gaps. The distinction prevents a searchable inventory from masquerading as a complete behavioral proof.

### F01 — Proxy.Close does not establish worker completion

**Protected behavior:** real-network delay/drop/rebinding fixtures should stop their own background work before releasing test state. [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go:226](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go#L226) closes a signal, currently known outgoing sockets and timers, then returns without joining the main reader, incoming queue consumer, outgoing queue consumer or nested outgoing reader. [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go:266](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go#L266) can read a packet and create a client mapping after that close pass; it has no terminal-admission check. Both [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go:33](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go#L33) and [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go:355](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy.go#L355) use blocking channel sends with no shutdown alternative, while their consumers can leave on `closeChan`. Caller cleanup later closes the listening socket, but neither socket close nor signaling establishes that all callbacks have finished. A read blocked in the listening socket before cleanup is not by itself proof of a leak: that socket is caller-owned and is normally closed by the caller.

**Evidence and confidence:** high confidence in the absent join and independently reachable source paths; medium confidence in shutdown-race consequences under a particular fixture schedule. No new deadlock reproduction or attribution to #44/#151/#169/#188/#241/#301/#320/#322 is claimed. The simulator's [testutils/simnet/simlink.go:76](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/testutils/simnet/simlink.go#L76) uses queue shutdown plus a wait group, demonstrating an existing, different completion boundary; this does not authorize replacing real UDP tests with it.

**Impact and recommendation:** late callbacks can outlive observations/cleanup and failed forwarding can silently terminate workers. B1 should define admission, caller-socket ownership and callback completion explicitly, then add a narrow shutdown mechanism and failure reporting. Preserve the socket-switch and packet timing/direction contracts. Check one pending read, one delayed-enqueue shutdown and one admission/close interleaving under deterministic coordination; stop after a discriminating old/new result per accepted behavior. A wrapper-level “started” signal alone does not prove a syscall is parked. Existing #317/#319 concern a different transport fixture and are precedent, not the same defect. No duplicate proxy-completion issue was found in the fetched repository issue titles.

### F02 — Fixture-owned sockets and two proxy readers are never closed

**Protected behavior:** each fixture releases its own client/accepted sockets and joins readers. [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go:114](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go#L114) creates a client with `net.DialUDP` without any close. The same omission appears in `TestDropIncomingPackets`, `TestDropOutgoingPackets`, `TestDelayIncomingPackets`, `TestPacketReordering`, `TestConstantDelay`, and `TestDelayOutgoingPackets`. [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go:208](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go#L208) and [integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go:391](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/proxy/proxy_test.go#L391) start indefinite `ReadFromUDP` loops on those client sockets and provide neither cancellation nor a join. `Proxy.Close` closes proxy-side sockets, not these distinct clients. A UDP peer's disappearance does not provide a reliable local read-unblock contract; the worker itself retains the socket.

The separate TLS cipher fixture [internal/qtls/cipher_suite_test.go:21](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/qtls/cipher_suite_test.go#L21) accepts a TCP/TLS connection, reads and reports completion without closing that accepted connection. Its dialed client closes on the success path only. Listener close does not close accepted connections. This is distinct from the repaired certificate tests covered by #80, whose current fixtures register cleanup and join their server workers.

**Evidence and confidence:** high, from complete function and cleanup-path inspection; no new repeated-run or descriptor-count measurement. The two proxy reader cases provide a concrete retained-worker mechanism, not just missing stylistic `defer` statements. Other listed sockets lack deterministic ownership cleanup even if garbage collection eventually reclaims them.

**Impact and recommendation:** B2 should register cleanup immediately after successful acquisition and join the two readers after local socket close, including assertion-failure paths. Preserve packet payload/count/order assertions and selected TLS cipher checks. Two child-fixture cleanup regressions (proxy and cipher ownership) should check acquired resource closure and worker completion; no broad global goroutine-count oracle is needed. B2 is independent of B1 but should precede any proxy stress work. #80 is related prior repair, not authority to reopen its completed scope.

### F03 — Fatal assertions escape the test goroutine

**Protected behavior:** fixture failures should reach the owning test with useful errors while workers still finish their teardown. The local Go toolchain's `testing.FailNow` contract requires the test goroutine and uses Goexit. `require.*` calls that fail invoke that boundary. [integrationtests/self/handshake_test.go:711](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/handshake_test.go#L711) calls `require.NoError` in an accept worker before a non-deferred `close(acceptDone)`: failure loses the completion notification and the parent reports a second timeout. [integrationtests/self/http_test.go:975](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/http_test.go#L975) asserts in a separately launched accept worker; [integrationtests/self/http_test.go:851](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/http_test.go#L851) asserts inside an HTTP handler before publishing its result. [integrationtests/self/http_shutdown_test.go:23](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/http_shutdown_test.go#L23) launches a closure with a fatal assertion and uses a real-time sleep as a shutdown-start assumption. [integrationtests/self/multiplex_test.go:21](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/multiplex_test.go#L21), [integrationtests/self/nat_rebinding_test.go:82](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/nat_rebinding_test.go#L82), [internal/qtls/cipher_suite_test.go:21](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/qtls/cipher_suite_test.go#L21) and [send_stream_test.go:1426](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/send_stream_test.go#L1426) have further concrete worker-side require calls. The inventory's lexical worker-fatal markers are candidate sites, not a complete callback escape analysis; handler callbacks demonstrate why `go func` searching alone misses cases.

**Evidence and confidence:** high confidence in execution context for these inspected sites; failure occurrence and whether an individual case hangs, times out, or merely loses attribution are not established. Fatal assertions mark the test failed; this finding does not claim failures silently pass. Some workers already defer completion, so their consequence differs from GetConfigForClient. Ordinary `assert`/`t.Error`, helpers invoked synchronously by a test, and callbacks actually run by `t.Run`/`synctest.Test` are not automatically defects.

**Recommendation:** B3 should return errors/results to the owning test, ensure completion publication on every exit, and bound joins after cancellation/close. Begin with shared multiplexing and HTTP fixture paths; coordinate the cipher change with B2. Preserve actual retry, context, shutdown and retransmission assertions. Propose one injected worker error and one successful completion per changed helper owner; do not rewrite every assertion mechanically. #314/#315 are completed retry-handoff repairs and do not resolve these sites or establish the historical #151 timeout's cause.

### F04 — Greased middle-position check cannot detect its intended defect

**Protected behavior:** [internal/protocol/version_test.go:127](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/protocol/version_test.go#L127) attempts to observe reserved-version insertion at the first, last and middle positions while preserving supported-version order. Inside the reserved-version branch, `greasedVersionMiddle++` runs unconditionally, including first and last positions. Consequently the final `require.NotZero(greasedVersionMiddle)` is satisfied whenever a reserved version is found, even if the implementation only inserts at the endpoints. The other checks still protect length, reservation bits and original ordering; the entire test is not meaningless.

**Evidence and confidence:** high, source-confirmed control-flow defect; no mutation executed. A finite future sensitivity check that allows only endpoints should fail the middle assertion after correction and pass it before correction, while preserving the original sequence checks. B4 should correct the actual position predicate and decide deterministic case selection together with F05. No existing greasing-assertion issue was found in the fetched issue titles.

### F05 — Statistical observations are mandatory success predicates without a recorded decision rule

**Protected behavior:** the suite should detect broken generation without treating every possible valid random sample as a defect. [internal/utils/rand_test.go:9](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/utils/rand_test.go#L9) requires the average of 1,000 crypto-backed bounded random values to be within `max/25` of `max/2`; [internal/utils/rand.go:14](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/utils/rand.go#L14) obtains fresh crypto randomness. Valid bounded samples can fall outside that interval. [internal/protocol/version_test.go:127](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/protocol/version_test.go#L127) also requires both endpoint positions to appear in 100 random insertions and limits duplicates; [internal/ackhandler/packet_number_generator_test.go:55](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/internal/ackhandler/packet_number_generator_test.go#L55) requires sample averages of randomized skip periods to meet fixed tolerances. These are intentionally distribution-related tests, unlike random payload round-trips whose assertions hold for every supported payload.

**Evidence and confidence:** high that these pass/fail predicates depend on samples; no estimated suite flake rate, empirical incident or common cause with packet-loss failures is claimed. A sampling test may be justified by an explicit false-rejection budget. This audit found none in these test bodies; it does not establish that every statistical test is inherently invalid or that any threshold should simply be loosened.

**Recommendation:** in B4, retain exact range/order properties and choose a small controlled corpus or an explicit, justified statistical-test policy for distribution checks. That choice belongs to the repair's contract; do not globally reseed production randomness or relax thresholds merely to pass. One forced valid extreme sample can distinguish sampling rejection from an implementation bug; a deliberately biased/broken generator should remain detectable. No random repetition campaign is proposed. #145 is prior evidence for separating mandatory guarantees from exploration, not proof these generators share its cause.

### F06 — Tests can pass without establishing that the read was already blocked

**Protected behavior:** W1's native read tests claim deadline updates and closure interrupt an already-blocked read. [sys_conn_closure_test.go:105](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/sys_conn_closure_test.go#L105) launches `readPacketAsync`, sleeps 50 ms, then changes the deadline/closes the socket. The helper exposes only the final result, no observation that the relevant read path has reached its blocking boundary. If the worker is first scheduled after the deadline/close, it can return the expected error without exercising interruption of an outstanding read. [sys_conn_closure_test.go:131](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/sys_conn_closure_test.go#L131) varies a delay over 20 iterations but does not establish which interleaving occurred.

**Evidence and confidence:** high confidence in missing precondition evidence; medium confidence as a meaningful coverage risk. No failure of native polling or product close semantics is established. The tests do exercise error delivery and some real scheduling; retaining them is preferable to deleting coverage. The related sustained-transfer and write-after-close cases have distinct assertions and are not included in this concern.

**Recommendation:** B5 should define the deepest existing observable readiness seam and preserve native plus basicConn coverage. If an exact parked-syscall observation requires a new production hook or stronger guarantee, stop for that design decision rather than labeling a pre-call notification as proof. One coordinated blocked-read case and one “late-start worker” negative control should bound the repair; platform expansion needs its own justification. Existing native qualification evidence remains unchanged.

### F07 — Optional transport qlogs collide within one second

**Protected behavior:** optional diagnostics should retain distinct transport histories. [integrationtests/tools/qlog.go:20](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/tools/qlog.go#L20) builds `log_<second-resolution timestamp>_transport.qlog` and opens it with `os.Create`. Multiple transport tracers started in one second in the same directory open the same pathname, truncating it and potentially writing through separate buffered writers. [integrationtests/self/self_test.go:174](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/self_test.go#L174) calls this helper for each traced transport; [integrationtests/self/multiplex_test.go:150](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/blob/07d8716ddd00b193f2c331d443a123c62a516cd6/integrationtests/self/multiplex_test.go#L150) creates multiple traced transports in one test. Connection qlogs use a different connection-ID-based naming scheme, so this particular claim is limited to transport files.

**Evidence and confidence:** high confidence in the pathname/truncation mechanism; no archived log corruption is claimed and normal CI has optional qlog disabled. The retained HTTP-capture system uses a different bounded directory/recording contract and is not implicated. #76 repaired production tracing ownership; this integration helper is a different owner.

**Recommendation:** B6 should use collision-resistant per-transport filenames in a fixture-owned directory and retain a bounded flush/join boundary. A two-tracer same-directory test must retain two independent parseable histories without replacing the packet/event semantics. This is lower priority than fixture completion and mandatory-oracle defects.

## Remaining coverage and confidence gaps

- Initial screening covers every maintained entry, but the per-entry `Q-semantic`, `Q-sensitive` or `Q-beyond-inspected-scope` records retain deeper questions. Assertion presence, current passing history and source names do not establish mutation sensitivity or completeness.
- The certificate tests under `internal/testdata` and `http3/internal/testdata` are maintained and inventoried. The ordinary `./...` package pattern skips testdata directories; the inspected current workflows do not explicitly invoke these two packages. Their repairs have prior explicit verification, but recurring CI coverage is not established. Deciding whether to add it is a separate CI-scope decision, not a change in this PR.
- Ten native fuzz entrypoints are inventoried; `.clusterfuzzlite/build.sh` lists seven. FuzzParsePriority, FuzzEncoder and FuzzFindSNI are not in that adapter list. Native seed tests and dedicated continuous fuzzing are different coverage. The pinned ClusterFuzzLite PR/batch/coverage workflows are dispatch-only, and CodSpeed is also dispatch-only. No external service/toolchain availability was verified and no policy change is recommended by inference alone.
- Windows, Linux, Darwin, privilege, race, FIPS and native capability differences are explicitly inventoried. Cross-compilation does not demonstrate runtime behavior. Platform capability skips and environment guards can legitimately narrow execution; actual engagement requires the existing native evidence, not counts of green test names.
- Frozen experiments, generated mocks and diagnostic infrastructure have explicit classifications. No attempt was made to recursively certify capture tools, prove all cryptographic/wire predicates, infer resource safety from a global count, or reopen completed evidence campaigns.
- New causal checks are proposals only. Audit extraction and consistency validation did not run the product test suite. Hosted publication checks retain their ordinary role and cannot establish absence of intermittent defects.
