## Outcome and boundaries

Deliver one evidence PR with an adopt/adapt/defer/reject assessment of a disposable 2× slow-start pacing candidate. Both measured sources derive from `1c4a28b7bc000bbc521332f5d7145e6493b42709`. The common prerequisite retains the callback-based pacer, moves its identical bytes-per-second rate expression into the synchronous sender owner, and adds identical observations. The candidate-only runtime difference is the slow-start branch. Tests and archived patches do not change shipped pacing. Preserve public API, congestion-window admission and growth, recovery, default selection, BBR, queue/byte-credit/burst constants, and packet-emission ownership. Production adoption, further campaigns, cross-platform qualification and BBR policy changes are outside the contract.

The source expression before and after the common prerequisite is `uint64(BandwidthEstimate()/BytesPerSecond)*5/4`; constructor budget initialization and pacer methods retain the same operations. Existing control tests pass. Gain selection uses current sender state with no mirrored phase or asynchronous hand-off. The candidate changes rate-dependent burst quantities by design; it does not change the formula or clamp those effects. The rejected upstream #5881 alternative threads rates through every pacer method and changes initialization and rounding responsibilities, adding scope without helping this comparison. Revisit only in a separately approved adoption/refactor proposal.

The ordinary UDP wrapper records successful socket writes. A synchronous bounded recorder stores sender observations and transport events in memory, exporting after each attempt. Both arms have identical overhead. The relay uses one event loop and owns service accounting and timed deliveries; it adds no state hand-off to the QUIC connection. Process readiness, cancellation and cleanup belong to the campaign runner. The owner's alternative of kernel traffic shaping would require host-setting changes and is excluded; revisit only with a separately authorized native emulator surface. Frozen historical helpers are reference material and remain unchanged.

## Representation, artifacts and terminating evidence

Supported inputs are the canonical finite configurations in registration.json, native IPv4 loopback UDP/TLS, the two payload sizes, two RTTs/rates, three impairment modes and declared seeds. Python's JSON parser and Go's typed records own the evidence representation; QUIC/TLS remains owned by quic-go and crypto/tls. Configuration guarantees are canonical-subset; performance conclusions are example-level on this host. Every started attempt means the finite ledger, reconciled with retained raw attempt records. No external grammar scanner or universal cross-platform claim is introduced.

The candidate, fixture, tests, analysis and plots are verification aids, retired and frozen at assessment completion. Their supported domain is this registration; the assessment author owns them. They are not maintained production gates. Deadline, capacity, attempt-limit and cleanup enforcement are required experiment safety enforcement. The plan, registration, reports, hashes and receipts are process/traceability metadata. No shipped runtime behavior is changed by this PR. Full contract closure is not triggered: focused semantic tests cover the isolated code, and fixture validation is not recursively promoted into a maintained-product obligation.

The finite semantic evidence covers real Reno/Cubic constructors, initial RTT and zero-RTT-stat fallback, initial burst and budget exhaustion, the 1.5625 ms deadline, both sides of the 1 ms floor, rate-dependent burst allowance, integer rounding/overflow, packet-size change, loss/HyStart exit, timeout/migration re-entry, and congestion-window admission. Existing congestion/ackhandler tests, focused race checks, BBR/real-packer emission preservation tests, vet and module tidiness complete local code evidence. Fixture evidence covers deterministic serialization, in-service capacity, overflow, impairments, timing components, counting and native shutdown. The network budget is 304 attempts including failures, at most 8 smoke and 8 replacements, 10 seconds per attempt, 2 hours preparation and 1 hour network stage. Four smoke attempts are scheduled; the unused four and eight replacements remain explicit reserves. There is no automatic second campaign or mutation/platform/fuzz expansion.

One fully briefed RAS review is required. The implementing agent dispositions findings and performs accepted fixes; targeted verification and at most one replacement review are permitted. Any required widening, source correction invalidating measured evidence, repeated precise enforcement root or other stop-for-decision preserves the branch and returns to the operator. No measurement rerun is implicitly authorized. The terminating outcome may be defer with explicit missing evidence; completeness is never manufactured by extra trials.
