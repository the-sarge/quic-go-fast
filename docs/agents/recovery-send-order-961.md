# Recovery ordering across packet-number spaces

The accepted implementation contract for [#961](https://github.com/the-sarge/quic-go-fast/issues/961#issuecomment-6331591220) is one atomic typed-callback, Reno/CE and HybridSlowStart correction, following [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-recovery-registration-prefix.md). Recovery remains the admission owner, the controller owns its eligible and accepted cutback boundaries, and HybridSlowStart owns its receive round. Registration precedes asynchronous socket I/O on the connection goroutine.

For admitted registered identities, the ordering guarantee is universal within the three normalized spaces; it does not validate arbitrary peer numbers. Presence and strict greater-than comparisons describe a complete prefix. An eligible send captures all earlier registrations, including excluded registrations in other spaces. Trailing excluded registrations remain after that frontier. Existing validation and disposal guards retain their enforcement owners.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Maintained tests reproduce the four original failures through the real recovery handler, preserve all four single-space controls, and cover overlapping Handshake/application numbering, a later valid loss and ACK, suppressed callbacks, initial/no-prior-ACK state and an old ACK after recovery exit. One old flight produces one cut; a genuinely later eligible flight can produce the next cut.
2. Prefix tests cover strict equality, absent spaces, shared 0/1-RTT identity, and excluded registrations both before and after the eligible frontier, including interleaving across spaces. They prove the frontier captures the complete registration prefix at the latest eligible send. ACK-only/path exclusions, MTU loss exclusion and eligible MTU ACK recovery exit remain correct without pacing/flight-accounting changes.
3. Real ECN-tracker tests establish capability and exercise a lower live ACK-only record with a higher ordinary packet retired through PTO extraction. With advancing-largest and nonempty-live guards satisfied, present and retired CE anchors classify identically before and after the frontier: suppress the old event and cut once for the new event. Duplicate/reordered feedback and mixed CE/loss do not double-cut. A mocked congestion decision alone is insufficient evidence.
4. HybridSlowStart accumulates the tested eight RTT samples across interleaved spaces and ends its receive round only for the appropriate eligible post-prefix ACK. Recovery cutbacks do not take ownership of that round boundary.
5. Maintained lifecycle coverage verifies Retry, Initial/Handshake discard, 0-RTT rejection, migration, intentional skips and pooled lifetimes. Existing BBR tests preserve ordinal sequence, delivery accounting, CE handling, undo/persistent-congestion spans and bounded stores. No preparatory BBR counter refactor is required.
6. Packet metadata has no growth attributable to this correction; snapshots have constant per-sender cost with no retained ledger. Record the final layout and target alongside the prototype measurement: Go 1.27.3 darwin/arm64, packet 80 → 80 bytes and Reno sender 208 → 352 bytes, including embedded HybridSlowStart (+144 bytes total, not an additional slow-start charge). This is evidence of prototype cost, not a mandatory production layout or a CPU/total-memory improvement claim. Account for initial Reno construction even when BBR is selected.
7. The production change records this accepted correctness decision in maintained domain/decision documentation and release notes. Reconcile ADR 0009/D04's “Reno unchanged” language with this narrow default-Reno correction while retaining Reno selection/parameters and avoiding mandatory per-packet BBR metadata. Preserve ADR 0001's public/wire/ownership contracts and ADR 0004's registration/I/O ownership; record the scoped ordering correction without reopening ADR 0003's upstream-selection policy. Frozen assessment and prototype captures remain unchanged.
8. The typed callbacks, prefix facts, Reno/CE and HybridSlowStart conversion land coherently, with no intermediate mixed raw cross-space ordering. Fresh maintained regressions and affected-package tests, race tests and vet pass at the implementation candidate's HEAD; existing relevant emission/coalescing, 0-RTT/TLS integration checks and repository CI pass as affected by the final change. Archived replay fixtures are reference evidence, not the maintained CI input. No broad benchmark or native-platform campaign is required for this issue.

## Evidence and boundaries

Maintained real-handler tests cover the eight paired regressions, Handshake/application overlap, real-tracker present/retired CE anchors, mixed CE/loss, lifecycle disposal, skips and eligibility. Controller tests cover complete-prefix freezing, suppressed callbacks, initialization and recovery exit; HybridSlowStart tests cover interleaved RTT samples and round boundaries. These tests are maintained verification aids owned by their packages, required by this contract, and replaceable by equivalent coverage when implementation changes. The archived replay drivers and prototype patch are frozen reference evidence, not CI inputs.

Run affected-package and root tests, recovery/congestion race tests, vet on changed packages, generated-output checks, formatting, module tidiness, and existing emission/coalescing and 0-RTT/TLS/migration integration coverage. Record target-specific layout with no per-packet growth and constant per-sender storage. Existing hosted unit, integration, lint and cross-compilation gates apply. No additional mutation, fuzz, statistical repetition, native qualification or performance campaign is required. Full contract-closure escalation is not triggered: the finite admitted states have direct focused coverage without new ownership or asynchronous paths.

Runtime code is shipped behavior; admission and disposal guards are required safety enforcement; tests and generated mocks are verification aids; plans, layout records and review receipts are traceability metadata. No recursive completeness requirement is imposed on the evidence aids.
